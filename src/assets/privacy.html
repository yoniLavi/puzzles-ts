Privacy

Hintful Puzzles keeps nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no personal information is collected or stored, here or anywhere else.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, saved games, checkpoints and preferences live in your own browser’s storage, on your device, and are never sent anywhere. Delete them at any time from the preferences, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Measurement

The app may count how it is used in aggregate: how many people played a puzzle, or which features get used. Any such counting records anonymous actions only. It carries no personal information, and it uses no cookies and no identifier of any kind stored on your device.

Crash reports

When a build of the app has crash reporting switched on, an unexpected error sends a report to the developer so the bug can be fixed. The report describes the error, the version of the app, and the browser and screen it happened on. It does not include your identity, your games or anything you have saved, and personal information is switched off in the reporting on purpose.

Other sites

Links to other websites (the puzzles’ originals, app stores, the source code on GitHub) leave this app, and those sites have their own policies.
